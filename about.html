My Story & Background

Engineering AI That Actually Ships

From experimenting with microcontrollers and circuits to architecting enterprise-scale LLMs and real-time vision pipelines.

4+ Years Production AI Experience

Bridging the Gap Between Research & Production

My journey into Artificial Intelligence began four years ago. While studying for my degree in Electrical and Electronics Engineering, I started tinkering with machine learning models as a passionate hobby. What started as curiosity soon became an obsession.

I quickly realized that while many could train models in notebooks, very few could take complex research and engineer it into robust, production-scale software. I fell in love with solving that exact engineering challenge — scaling computer vision for edge devices, building low-latency LLM agents, and developing real-time voice AI systems. That passion turned into my full-time career.

What makes me different is that I don’t just train models. I architect and ship complete AI products that solve real business problems end to end.

My Mission

To help companies, founders, and engineering teams translate ambitious AI concepts into reliable, production-ready systems that deliver tangible business value from day one.
